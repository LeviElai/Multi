MULTIGAME ? Reading, result clarity, restored characters and performance

Scope
Preserved the original mode environments, canonical mode icons and final stage.
Restored the original champion geometry from Git f83b726. Curated 10 hair shapes,
8 hair colors, 8 faces, 8 shirt designs/colors, 5 bottom designs with 8 colors,
8 hats plus none, 3 accessories plus none and 8 skin tones. Saved old looks remain
readable. All clients retain the same ID-bound appearance and secure lobby editing.
A 32-character contact sheet was reviewed at 32px and 48px display widths.

Timing and scoring
Automatic rounds begin with five seconds of reading; hosts may choose 8 or 12
seconds in the lobby. The choice persists through restarts and following rounds.
The server rejects early answers and starts the full answer timer after reading.
Reading can be paused/resumed, advanced or skipped. Stimulus/answer controls stay
hidden during reading. Results share a 5.5-second authoritative reveal deadline:
answer 2s, comparison 2s, points 1.5s. Standings last 10 seconds, hold immutable
previous totals first, reveal gains, count to authoritative new totals, then reorder
by stable participant ID. Pauses retain progress; reconnects use the current phase.
Round-best ties are distinct from overall rank and the current player's marker.
Zero-point rounds receive no round-best winner. Final rounds go straight to the
approved podium, with the final score update integrated there.

Presentation
Sunset harbor homepage with Join and Create as the main actions; internal practice
routes retained. Quiet original pixel meadow replaces the global dark pattern.
Eight stable ID-derived quiz-card colors, single title, actual question count.
Reduced repeated score panels, repeated titles and generic instructional copy.

Performance
Unchanged snapshot revisions no longer redraw the game tree. Polling falls from
1.1s to 1.8s, or 8s with a socket, and 15s in background tabs. Concurrent refreshes
are coalesced. Characters and atmosphere are memoized. Standings batch geometry
reads before transform writes. Clocks update once per second rather than four.
All six mode backgrounds are no longer prefetched on every page. Ambient glow is
static; small screens show fewer particles and drop backdrop filters. Count uses
squared-distance rejection before square roots, caps rendering at 30fps on narrow
or low-core devices, and retains non-overlapping stacking with a bounded 128 bodies.
Local single-run benchmark, same 128 bodies/60 physics steps: 249.8ms before,
62.6ms after. This measures CPU work on this PC, not physical-phone FPS.
