MULTIGAME character and sound assets

The character system is original deterministic SVG artwork on a 32 by 40 integer
pixel grid. It uses modular skin, hair, face, clothing, hats and shoes. Artwork
is generated locally by PlayerIdentity.tsx; no external character images, branded
characters, runtime image-generation service or downloaded sprite packs are used.
Crowns are a temporary winner status and are not a selectable accessory.

The sound palette is original Web Audio oscillator synthesis in apps/web/lib/audio.ts.
There are no downloaded audio files or new audio-license dependencies. Mode pitch
variations share the existing short chip melodies. Per-event deduplication,
voice limits, persistent mute and hidden-tab silence apply. No new background
music was added. Existing pixel backgrounds and canonical mode logos are retained.

CSS ambient fish, gears, pages, material patterns and conveyor details are authored
in the repository. Reduced motion disables decorative animation; hidden tabs pause
it, and low-core/save-data devices receive fewer ambient particles.
